NVIDIA
Defending the AI Infrastructure Monopoly
What must NVIDIA do in the next 12 months as AMD, custom silicon, and open-source inference erode its moat?
DAY 1: Bundle DGX Cloud, NIM, and CUDA into 3-year AI factory contracts, locking 50+ Fortune 500 accounts before hyperscaler custom silicon ships externally.
The "lock customers in" thesis was killed — hyperscalers already have custom silicon in production and NVIDIA's software platform couldn't generate defensible lock-in independent of GPU hardware advantage.
4 of 8 branches killed
REVISED
Ship Vera Rubin to hyperscalers by Q4 2025, price below custom ASIC TCO at scale, and accept software margin loss to hold training dominance.
EUROPEAN AUTOMAKERS
Surviving the Chinese EV Price War
What actions must European automakers take in the next 18 months — restructure manufacturing, partner with Chinese battery suppliers, or retreat to luxury?
DAY 1: Abandon mass-market EVs entirely, cede the sub-€35K segment to BYD and SAIC, concentrate on luxury EVs and autonomous driving.
The "retreat to luxury" thesis was partially killed — the luxury segment can't absorb redirected capital (Mercedes EQS sales dropped 50%), and ceding sub-€35K triggers a volume death spiral that destroys supplier economics.
6 of 10 branches killed
REVISED
Exit sub-€35K, partner with Chinese tech firms on software stacks, defend €35K-55K mid-market with platform-shared EVs.
WORKDAY
Defending HCM Dominance
What must Workday do in the next 18 months as Rippling commoditizes core HR/payroll and buyers demand AI-first workflows?
DAY 1: Acquire 2-3 AI-native workflow companies, rebuild core platform around agent-based automation by Q3 2027, convert to per-outcome pricing.
The most aggressive stress-test. Acquisitions unaffordable (40-60x revenue). Platform rebuild unrealistic (18 months on a 20-year codebase). Per-outcome pricing had no actuarial basis. Only one branch survived: the unified HCM-finance data layer.
13 of 14 branches killed
REVISED
Instrument the data layer with measurable customer-specific metrics and sell the data advantage, not the workflow.
