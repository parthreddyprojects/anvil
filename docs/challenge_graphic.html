Challenges to your hypothesis
  • DGX Cloud already bundles hardware + software + managed inference. If the pivot is "sell AI factories," the question is why the existing bundle isn't locking customers in. Azure and GCP offer NVIDIA hardware with their own orchestration layers — the lock-in may be at the cloud layer, not the chip layer.
  • Hyperscaler custom silicon is already in production. Google TPU v6 and Amazon Trainium2 are running internal workloads now. Q3 2027 may be the date they reach external customers, but the competitive pressure on NVIDIA's margins starts today.
  • Open-source inference is closing the software gap faster than expected. vLLM and TGI are reducing the performance premium that makes TensorRT-LLM a lock-in lever. Bundling software that competitors can replicate is not a durable moat.
For this hypothesis to hold, these must be true
  • Enterprise buyers prefer single-vendor AI stacks over best-of-breed
  • CUDA and NIM switching costs survive hardware commoditization
  • Custom silicon doesn't reach price-performance parity for fine-tuning before Q3 2027
