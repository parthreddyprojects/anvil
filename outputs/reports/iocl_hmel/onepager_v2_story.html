Crisis context for non-specialist readers: Iran mined the Strait of Hormuz on February 28, 2026, halting 20 million barrels/day of global crude — the largest supply shock in history. India's crude import cost has jumped from $69 to $156 per barrel. India imports 88% of its crude; 42% came through Hormuz. A US OFAC (sanctions enforcement) carve-out that allowed India to pay for Russian crude in rupees expires today, April 3, 2026 — simultaneously closing India's cheapest alternative just as Hormuz crude disappears.

Situation
Crude costs ₹32 more per barrel than frozen retail prices allow state refiners IOCL and Bathinda refiner (HMEL) to recover — burning ₹850 crore every day with no internal fix available.

A shutdown at Bathinda cuts supply to 12 million households; southern cooking-gas rationing will be visible to voters within 10 days.

The waiver permitting Russian crude payments expires tonight (April 3). The UAE is the only remaining non-Hormuz, non-sanctioned supplier with a cargo physically reachable within Bathinda's 8-day window. That cargo window closes in 24 hours.
Three independent failures all detonate on April 13 — payment first, then crude, then gas
1
The payment system breaks before physical crude runs out.
Banks will refuse to guarantee crude purchases by April 13, triggering automatic default on the full state-enterprise debt stack (IOCL balance sheet, March 31: ₹2,16,700 crore).
So what → Finance Secretary to authorize emergency LC headroom at State Bank of India today, April 3, by 18:00 IST — before cargo decisions below become void.
2
One cargo can reach Bathinda before shutdown — and only one.
A single UAE cargo, loaded by April 5, delivers 600,000 barrels — 11 days of Bathinda's requirement. Missing this window extends any restart from two weeks to six weeks, costing an additional ₹4,000 crore in idle-capacity losses. Every alternative supply route (West Africa, US Gulf) arrives after Bathinda's 8-day crude runway expires.
So what → MoPNG Secretary must sign UAE cargo authorization by April 4, 08:00 IST — every alternative supply route arrives after Bathinda's shutdown date.
3
Standard approval takes 5–10 days; the market window is 24 hours.
A standard approval cycle wastes ₹4,250 crore and loses the West African cargo window entirely.
So what →Without delegated authority in writing by April 3, the crisis task force cannot legally sign a single contract.
