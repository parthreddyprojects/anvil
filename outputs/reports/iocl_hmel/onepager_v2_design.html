Analysis — Why This Cannot Wait
01
The payment system breaks before the physical crude runs out.
Banks will refuse to guarantee crude purchases by April 13, triggering automatic default on the full state-enterprise debt stack (IOCL balance sheet: ₹2,16,700 crore).
Authorize emergency credit headroom to State Bank of India and Bank of Baroda today — before the sourcing decisions below become irrelevant.
02
One cargo can reach Bathinda before shutdown: a single available UAE cargo, loaded by April 5, delivering 600,000 barrels — 11 days of Bathinda's requirement.
Missing that window extends any restart from two weeks to six weeks, costing ₹4,000 crore.
Sign the UAE cargo authorization by April 4, 08:00 — every other supply option arrives after the shutdown window closes.
03
Standard government approval takes 5–10 days; the market window is 24 hours.
A standard approval cycle wastes ₹4,250 crore and loses the West African cargo window entirely.
Without delegated authority in writing by April 3 (in 7 days), the crisis task force cannot legally sign a single contract.
