**VLCC availability:** ~15–20 VLCCs available for prompt USGC loading as of April 2025. **Rate spike risk is HIGH** if India, Korea, and European buyers compete simultaneously for Cape-route VLCCs. Source: Clarksons Research April 2025. **Confidence: MEDIUM.**

**Refinery compatibility:**
- **Jamnagar DTA/SEZ** (design range 26–45° API, 0.1–3.5%S; NCI 21.1): Can run WTI Midland at **100% substitution**. Eagle Ford (50° API) processable up to ~**65–70% of diet** before reformer/hydrocracker H₂ balance constrains further uplift. **DECISION IMPLICATION:** Procure WTI Midland immediately for Jamnagar (full substitution viable); limit Eagle Ford to a blending component at Vadinar and source medium-sour alternatives to fill the balance.
