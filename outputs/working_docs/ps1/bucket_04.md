## 4.4 SEZ REGULATORY FLEXIBILITY FOR DOMESTIC DIVERSION

**Bottom line: SEZ Act requires ≥50% export by value — this is a statutory minimum, NOT a 100% export obligation. Significant domestic sale is already permitted from the SEZ. A crisis carve-out exists but has not been formally tested.**

**Confirmed regulatory baseline:**
- **SEZ Act 2005 + DGFT Rules:** SEZ units must achieve **Net Foreign Exchange (NFE) positive** and export **≥50% of production by value** | Source: SEZ Act 2005; DGFT regulations | **HIGH** | Standing regulation
- This means up to **50% of SEZ production by value can already be sold domestically** (DTA sales), subject to applicable customs duty on DTA clearances
- DTA sale from SEZ attracts: Basic Customs Duty (BCD) + applicable GST on the deemed import value — this increases cost of domestic sale from SEZ but does NOT prohibit it

**Crisis/emergency provision:**
- **Section 53 of SEZ Act + Board of Approval (BoA) powers:** BoA (chaired by Commerce Secretary) can grant temporary relaxations under "public interest" provisions
- Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG) can invoke essential commodities/national emergency provisions to direct SEZ units toward domestic supply
- **Historical precedent:** During COVID-19 (March–April 2020), SEZ units were granted temporary DTA sale permissions for medical/essential goods — **but petroleum product-specific SEZ crisis waiver has NOT been publicly documented**
- **SEZ Development Commissioner contact:** The DC Kandla SEZ (jurisdictional authority for Jamnagar SEZ) can grant interim permissions subject to BoA ratification; **48-hour administrative processing is feasible if MoPNG provides written direction**

**DATA GAP:** Whether RIL has contacted the DC Kandla SEZ or MoPNG in the current crisis is not in available research. This is an immediate action item.

**Financial impact of DTA sale from SEZ:**
- Customs duty on DTA clearance: ~5–7.5% of assessed value on petroleum products = **~$7–12/bbl cost penalty** at $156/bbl basis
- This penalty partially offsets the domestic sale premium over export net-back
- **EXTRAPOLATED from standard customs tariff structure | MEDIUM confidence**

**DECISION IMPLICATION:** RIL's CFO and Head of Refining should immediately confirm the current DTA/export split at SEZ, calculate whether the existing 50% DTA allowance is already being fully utilized, and simultaneously dispatch a formal request to DC Kandla SEZ + MoPNG for emergency DTA expansion permission — the customs duty penalty is recoverable if domestic IPP premium is sufficient.
