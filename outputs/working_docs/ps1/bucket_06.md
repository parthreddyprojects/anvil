## 6.1 — HORMUZ REOPENING: SCENARIO PROBABILITIES & WORKING ASSUMPTION

**WORKING ASSUMPTION FOR ALL PROCUREMENT DECISIONS: Scenario 2 (30-60 day negotiated resolution). Do not build strategy on rapid resolution.**

**Three-scenario probability framework:**

**Scenario A — Rapid Diplomatic Resolution (≤7 days): 15–20% probability**
- Requires: US-Iran Oman-channel backchannel produces immediate IRGC stand-down; minefield coordinates shared with USN
- Critical constraint: **Even with political resolution Day 1, physical mine clearance of primary commercial lane = minimum 21–30 days per USN MCM doctrine** [HIGH | Source: NATO Naval Mine Warfare doctrine; USN NAVSEA MCM estimates, 2023]

- Conditions: One lane partially cleared; Saudi/UAE diplomatic pressure on Iran; IRGC willing to share partial mine data
- Historical analog: **Gulf of Suez mining (1984) — 19 vessels damaged; multinational clearance took ~3 months for primary lanes** [HIGH | Source: Lloyd's Register historical; Royal Navy mine warfare records, C03]
- Historical analog: **Iran-Iraq War (1987–88) — mine clearance took 4–8 months for full high-traffic lane restoration** [HIGH | Source: USNI Proceedings; CNO Historical Reports, C03]
- Partial lane restoration in 30–60 days is achievable but **requires active Iranian cooperation** — this is the modal scenario
- [LOW confidence on probability | MEDIUM confidence on physical timeline | Source: C03]
