## 2.10 — CRUDE SWAP WITH RELIANCE/NAYARA: REGULATORY PATHWAY

**Bottom line: A PSU-to-private crude swap is commercially logical and operationally feasible — Reliance and Nayara hold Atlantic basin contracts and process complex crudes that PSUs cannot — but no standing regulatory mechanism exists; MoPNG must use emergency Essential Commodities Act authority to direct the exchange.**

**Commercial Logic:**
- Reliance Jamnagar (SEZ + DTA, combined 1.24 MMbpd) and Nayara Vadinar (400 kb/d) are among the world's most complex refineries (Nelson Complexity Index ~12–14 for Reliance). They are designed for heavy, sour crude diets and hold Atlantic basin and Latin American supply contracts (Brazilian Tupi/Lula, US WTI Midland, West African grades). Source: RIL Annual Report 2023–24; Nayara investor documents 2024. **Confidence: HIGH.**
- **Brazilian Tupi/Lula** (28–30° API, 0.40–0.77% S) trades at Dated Brent −$1.50 to −$0.50/bbl discount and is compatible with Reliance/Nayara complex trains. Source: Argus Q1 2025. **Confidence: HIGH.**
- **Swap structure:** Reliance/Nayara surrender lighter, sweeter Atlantic-basin cargo (e.g., Bonny Light, WTI Midland) to PSU refineries; in exchange receive government-allocated heavy Basrah/Kuwait crude (PSU term contract volumes) that they can process. Net result: both parties receive crude better suited to their CDU configuration.

**Regulatory Pathway:**
- **No standing MoPNG mechanism** exists for inter-refinery crude swaps between PSU and private entities under normal conditions. Source: PNGRB Act; Petroleum Act framework review. **Confidence: HIGH.**
- **Emergency pathway:** Section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act empowers Central Government to regulate production, supply, distribution, and disposal of petroleum products including crude oil in emergency conditions. A ministerial order under ECA could direct Reliance and Nayara to participate in an emergency crude exchange with PSU refineries. **Confidence: MEDIUM — legal interpretation; no precedent for private crude compulsion.**
- **Voluntary commercial route (faster and less contested):** MoPNG offers Reliance/Nayara: (a) priority allocation of heavy sour term crude from Aramco/ADNOC at below-market prices, and (b) regulatory goodwill in return for voluntary surrender of lighter grades. This requires no legal compulsion and could be executed in 48–72 hours via commercial LOI. **EXTRAPOLATED — MEDIUM confidence.**
- Timeline for voluntary swap execution: Commercial LOI 48 hours → formal cargo transfer instruction 24 hours → **physical crude available to PSU refineries within 5–7 days** (if cargo already in Reliance/Nayara tank farm).

**DECISION IMPLICATION:** MoPNG should approach Reliance and Nayara within 24 hours for a voluntary commercial swap before invoking ECA compulsion — both private refiners have strong incentives to cooperate given the reputational and regulatory environment of a national crisis.
