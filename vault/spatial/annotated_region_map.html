Three chokepoints define India's energy vulnerability
Gulf region · critical maritime infrastructure
Risk zones
1
Strait of Hormuz — 21 km at narrowest 20.5 mb/d transit (20% of global supply). Mined by Iran, US MCM ops underway.
2
